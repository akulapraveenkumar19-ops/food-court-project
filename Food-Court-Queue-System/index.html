How The Smart Queue Works

Experience contactless food ordering from selection to tray pickup with four simple steps.

1

Explore Menu

Browse appetizing dishes across 8 food categories with prices, veg/non-veg tags, and descriptions.

2

Add to Cart & Order

Customize quantities, provide your name and phone number, and choose your preferred payment mode.

3

Receive Token

Get a unique auto-generated token (e.g. #FC105) with your estimated waiting time and queue position.

4

Pick Up Meal

Track your status live on the screen. When your token turns "Ready", collect your meal at the counter.

Why Food Courts Love Our System

Designed to eliminate crowding, reduce order errors, and streamline kitchen workflow.

Zero Waiting Confusion

FIFO Queue Structure

Utilizes computer science First-In-First-Out (FIFO) queue principles so every customer is served fairly.

Admin Kitchen Display
